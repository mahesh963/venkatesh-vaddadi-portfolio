About

Full lifecycle, not just feature work.

I'm Venkatesh Vaddadi, a Senior React Native and React.js Developer with over eight years of experience building high-quality, scalable cross-platform mobile applications for iOS and Android — 6.9 years hands-on in React Native, including technical leadership on multi-role marketplace and consumer-ordering platforms, plus 1+ years focused on React.js frontend and API-integration work.

I've worked across event-ticketing marketplaces, large-scale restaurant ordering platforms, warehouse management, agribusiness loyalty programs, healthcare booking, travel planning and community apps — carrying each from UI and state management through testing, release, and App Store / Google Play deployment.

My day-to-day stack is TypeScript, JavaScript (ES6+) and React, with Redux, Redux-Thunk and Redux-Saga for state management, REST and GraphQL for data, and Jest, React Native Testing Library and Detox for testing.
